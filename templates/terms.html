The deal, in plain words

Last updated 8 November 2026. These terms match how the app behaves; where they ask something of you, the reason is in the sentence next to it.

What this is

Conceptly is a study tutor. It marks practice questions from past-paper banks, names the concept you appear to be struggling with, and teaches that concept on an animated whiteboard with spoken explanation. It is not a school, not an examination body, and not a replacement for a teacher.

Your account

  • Keep your password to yourself. If someone else signs in as you, that is on you, so tell us at once if you think it happened.
  • One account per person. Work spread across several accounts teaches the tutor nothing about you.
  • Give us your real form and date of birth — the syllabus and any consent requirement both depend on them.
  • You can close and delete everything yourself, from the Account page, at any time.

Do not

  • Upload papers you have no right to. If a document belongs to a school or an examination body, make sure you may study from it.
  • Use it to get answers during a live examination.
  • Try to break, probe, overload or impersonate the service, or harvest other students' data.
  • Resell access or put it behind your own paywall without asking first.

Marking is machine-assisted, and can be wrong

Answers are marked by a language model. It is good at the objective questions in the bank and genuinely shaky on long, handwritten, judgement-based work — where a marker wants method marks, wording, a diagram label or a unit. Treat every diagnosis as a strong suggestion, not a verdict, and never read a Conceptly score as a predicted KCSE or SAT result.

No warranty, and the limit of our responsibility

The app is provided as it is, without warranty of any kind, to the fullest extent the law allows. If it fails you, your remedy is to stop using it and delete your account. We are not liable for lost examination results, lost coursework, lost data or lost time, and our total liability for anything is limited to the amount you paid us — which today is nothing. That limitation does not extend to anything the law says it cannot, including our own negligence or a breach of data-protection duties.

Changes, and which law applies

If the app changes in a way these terms disagree with, we will tell you here before it affects you; carrying on means you accept them. Kenyan law governs, and the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 governs your data — see the Privacy Policy.
